ABOUT US

Crafting Luxury & Elegance

Established in 2019 by Rudi Handoko and based on the beautiful Batam Island, Indonesia, located in Villa Mas Blok B No. 7, Sungai Panas. We are a team of dedicated specialists in the field of interiors.

Our target market consists of high-end individuals who appreciate the finer things in life. We create bespoke interior pieces that elevate living spaces to new heights of luxury and sophistication.

Our Vision

To become the epitome of excellence in interior design within the Kepri region, transforming spaces into captivating works of art.

Our Mission

Create beauty and high-quality, deliver the best work products, and provide exceptional service from consultation to installation.

STEP BY STEP

Our Project Process

01

Meeting & Design Concept

Initial consultation to understand preferences, followed by custom 3D design creation and fine-tuning with client feedback.

02

Quotation & Material Selection

Transparent pricing assessment and comprehensive material breakdown presentation to ensure informed decisions.

03

Workshop Production & Delivery

Skilled craftsmen build elements in state-of-the-art workshops followed by secure, professional logistics delivery.

04

Installation, QC & Handover

On-site setup with strict Quality Control (QC) checks, final cleaning, touch-ups, and proud client handover.
